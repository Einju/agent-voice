How it works

1 · Claim your Agent ID

In the Binance App: Wallet → Agentic Wallet banner → claim your free name.ai identity. One per user, non-transferable.

2 · Connect & vote

Connect your wallet, pick a poll, cast your vote. The contract checks your Agent ID on-chain — one ID, one vote, no exceptions.

3 · Results are public

Every vote is an on-chain transaction anyone can verify. The contract holds no funds and has no owner or admin keys.
